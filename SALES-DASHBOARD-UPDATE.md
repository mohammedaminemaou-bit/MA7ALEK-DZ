# MA7ALAK DZ — Sales dashboard update

Added a professional Arabic RTL sales dashboard to the home screen.

## Included
- Today's net sales and transaction count.
- Month-to-date net sales and comparison against the same elapsed period in the previous month.
- Cash and credit sales summary.
- Estimated daily profit after recorded expenses (hidden for worker role).
- Seven-day sales bar chart with returns deducted.
- Cash/credit distribution visualization.
- Top products sold today.
- Responsive cards matching existing app themes.

## Notes
- Figures are computed from the app's existing local/synced data structures; no database schema changes were made.
- Profit remains an estimate based on stored purchase/sale costs and recorded expenses. Sales without a cost price cannot contribute a reliable profit figure.
- Please test with a non-production account before deploying to live shop data.
